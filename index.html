The Verdict Room

A group project based on the manipulation in the justice system and how simple decisions can have a huge impact to someone.

About

The player is a judge and must decide the fate of 15 inmates, depending on what is written in their documents. Your choices alter what ending you recieve at the end of the game.

Process

Art/Visuals: We had a dedicated 2D artist for this project and they did a great job.

Sound/Music: One of my teammates made the music but I was the one who had to edit all the voices for the game. This was done with Audacity, I added a distortion effect to make it seem like there was glass in the way, or the voice is coming through a telephone.

Code: This game was a huge step up from anything I had made before, many challenges were overcome. I used many online reasorces to reach our goal, Unity forums, Stackoverflow and YouTube tutorials were my main sources of information. I am extremely proud of myself and everything I managed to do in this game, I often go back and look and the code I wrote to partially re-use in other projects.
